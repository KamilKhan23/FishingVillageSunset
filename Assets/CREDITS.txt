CREDITS - Fishing Village at Sunset (CSE-411 Mini Project 1)
=============================================================

No external asset packs, models, textures, skyboxes or audio were imported into this project.
Every model, texture, material and the skybox in the scene was created for this project
(by the editor tools in Assets/Editor and the shader in Assets/Shaders).

   Licence: Unity Companion License
